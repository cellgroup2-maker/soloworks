Privacy policy

How SoloWorks (operated by UD IMPACT Inc.) handles the personal information you send with a quote request. Effective 22 October 2026.

1. What we collect

  • Required: name, company, email, your request
  • Optional: phone number, timeline
  • Automatically: basic details such as the page and time of a submission, used to prevent spam. We don’t use advertising trackers.

While you fill in the form, your answers are saved only in your own browser. They are removed once you send the form.

2. Why we use it

Only to reply with a quote, talk about your project, and, if you go ahead, to contract and deliver the work.

3. How long we keep it

For 1 year after our conversation ends, then we delete it. If you become a client, we keep contract records for as long as the law requires. If you ask us to delete your information, we do so without delay.

4. Service providers

We use cloud providers to store requests, send email and alert our team. They process the information only for these purposes.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or ask us to stop using it, at any time. Write to thlee@udimpact.ai.

6. Contact

UD IMPACT Inc. · Donhwamun-ro, Jongno-gu, Seoul · thlee@udimpact.ai

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the effective date at the top of this page.
