FAQ

Good to know before you start

Anything else? Just ask in the form below.

Who will I work with?

One dedicated SoloWorks PM handles your project in Korean from request to delivery. You never coordinate with individual freelancers.

How do I pay?

By card. The card receipt is issued instantly, so you can use it as proof of payment right away.

How can the prices be this reasonable?

Standardized processes cut out unnecessary costs. Prices stay low, and your PM checks quality before every delivery.

How is pricing set?

Starting from the prices above, your PM gets in touch and tells you the price for your request right away. Work begins as soon as you agree, and nothing extra is charged without your consent.

What if the result does not match the brief?

We check every deliverable against your brief before delivery, and fixes within scope are free.

What do I receive?

Finished work that matches your brief, in the file formats you need. We manage the process, so you only review the result.
